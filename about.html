About Home Reno Hotspot

One licensed plumber for a Columbus water heater replacement.

Home Reno Hotspot is a service of Home Reno Hotspot LLC. We introduce homeowners in Columbus, Ohio who need a water heater replaced to one licensed plumber. Homeowners pay us nothing. The plumber pays a referral fee only if the homeowner confirms them.

How it works

  • You tell us the problem. Leaking, no hot water, or a planned replacement, plus your zip code.
  • We qualify the request. We ask whether you own the home and, if you send one, read the heater label.
  • You confirm one plumber. Nothing is introduced until you reply YES to that named plumber.
  • You talk through us. Calls and texts use a Home Reno Hotspot number and may be recorded.

Service area

Columbus, Ohio. Plumbers apply at partners.homerenohotspot.com/apply.
