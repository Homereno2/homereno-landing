What is Home Reno Hotspot?

Home Reno Hotspot introduces Columbus homeowners who need a water heater replaced to one licensed plumber. You pay us nothing. The plumber pays a referral fee only if you confirm them.

How it works

  1. Tell us the problem — leaking, no hot water, or a planned replacement, plus your zip code.
  2. We qualify — we ask whether you own the home and read a label photo if you send one.
  3. You confirm one plumber — reply YES before that plumber contacts you.
  4. You talk through us — calls and texts use a Home Reno Hotspot number.

SMS messages

Message frequency: Message frequency varies. Texts are about your water heater request, scheduling, and the plumber you confirm.

Rates: Msg & data rates may apply.

STOP: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from SMS. You will receive a confirmation message.

HELP: Reply HELP for assistance or visit this page anytime.
