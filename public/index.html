How the utility works

We ask whether you own the home, which zip code the heater is in, and whether it is leaking, out of hot water, or a planned replacement. A label photo lets us read the brand, fuel, and age. You then confirm one licensed plumber. Until you do, that plumber does not receive your phone number.
