Homeowners

Share scope by message. Get one exclusive match.

Start with your name and phone number, then continue the conversation over native messaging — SMS or RCS — to describe your project and share reference photos.

  • Low-friction photo and scoping drop via messaging
  • Exclusive connection to a credential-verified local pro
  • No bidding wars or multi-contractor spam
  • Phone numbers handled through our masked relay — not sold or exposed for resale
Contractors

License and COI verification. Exclusive matching access.

Contractors enroll through a separate onboarding path. We verify licensing and certificate of insurance (COI) credentials before granting access to matched homeowner requests.

  • Credential verification against submitted license and COI documentation
  • Exclusive, scoped matches — not shared lead lists
  • Coordination through the platform relay system
  • Subscription terms provided at contractor enrollment

How the utility works

We collect the information needed to scope a renovation request — contact details, project description, and reference photos you choose to send — then use automated and human-assisted systems to qualify the request and coordinate a single introduction.

Communications run through SMS and RCS where supported. Reply STOP to opt out of messages or HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.
