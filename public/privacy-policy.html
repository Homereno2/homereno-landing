Introduction

Home Reno Hotspot (“we,” “us,” “our”) operates a scheduling and verification utility that helps homeowners and contractors coordinate renovation project introductions in the New York City metro area. This Privacy Policy describes what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

This policy applies when you use our website, SMS, RCS (Rich Communication Services), or other messaging channels we support. By submitting your information or using our service, you consent to the practices described here. Questions may be directed to the contact information at the end of this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to scope requests, verify credentials, and coordinate introductions:

  • Contact information: Name, phone number, and email address when you provide them.
  • Project scope: Descriptions of renovation work, location (such as neighborhood or borough), timeline preferences you share, and related details provided via form or messaging.
  • Reference photos: Images you voluntarily send to help describe project scope for matching and coordination purposes.
  • Communication data: Content and metadata of messages exchanged with our automated and human-assisted systems via SMS, RCS, or other supported channels.
  • Consent records: Documentation of your opt-in to receive SMS or RCS messages, where applicable.

What we do not collect through our public-facing service: We do not collect, generate, or process three-dimensional models, photogrammetry outputs, LiDAR scans, or spatial reconstruction data as part of our published homeowner or contractor coordination service.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Qualify and scope renovation requests submitted by homeowners.
  • Verify contractor licensing and certificate of insurance (COI) documentation submitted during enrollment.
  • Coordinate a single, scoped introduction between a homeowner and a credential-verified contractor.
  • Operate our masked relay communication system so parties can coordinate without direct exposure of personal phone numbers for unsolicited contact or resale.
  • Send service-related SMS or RCS messages about your request, scheduling, and coordination.
  • Respond to support inquiries, process opt-out requests, and comply with legal obligations.

Masked Relay Communication

Home Reno Hotspot uses a masked relay model for phone-based coordination. Homeowner and contractor phone numbers are not sold, rented, or published for third-party marketing. Numbers are not directly exposed to the other party for unsolicited off-platform contact as part of our standard coordination flow.

Service-related calls and messages are routed through our platform and messaging infrastructure so introductions can be scheduled and scoped without operating as a lead broker or selling contact data.

SMS and RCS Messaging

When you opt in to receive SMS or RCS messages from Home Reno Hotspot:

  • Consent: By providing your phone number and agreeing to receive texts (for example, via our lead form or by initiating contact through messaging), you consent to service-related SMS or RCS from Home Reno Hotspot. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP to any message to opt out. We will send a final confirmation and then cease SMS/RCS messages. Opting out of messaging does not automatically delete all stored data; see “Your Rights” below.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance or contact help@homerenohotspot.com.
  • No selling or marketing sharing: Your mobile phone number and opt-in data will not be sold or shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Limited use: Mobile information is used only for messages related to your renovation request, scheduling, coordination, and service updates.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

With matched contractors or homeowners

We share project scope information and coordination details necessary to facilitate a scoped introduction. Contact coordination occurs through our masked relay system; we do not sell or expose phone numbers for third-party marketing or lead resale.

With service providers

We may share information with infrastructure and messaging providers (such as hosting and SMS/RCS delivery partners) that help us operate the platform. These providers are contractually required to protect your information and use it only to perform services for us.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of our platform and users.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time (see “Your Rights”). We will process deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and operational requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements.
  • Opt out of SMS/RCS by replying STOP to any message.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting prior lawful processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

Our service is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy
