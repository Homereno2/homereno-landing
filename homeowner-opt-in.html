Overview

Home Reno Hotspot sends customer-care texts to homeowners who request a water heater replacement and opt in. Texts include qualification questions, scheduling updates, a request to confirm one named plumber, and messages from that plumber sent through Home Reno Hotspot. We do not send marketing or promotional texts on this program.

Ways Homeowners Consent to Receive SMS

Homeowners consent to receive SMS from Home Reno Hotspot in the following ways:

  • Website. The request form includes a separate checkbox that is optional and unchecked. The request can be submitted without it. Checking it and submitting the form is consent. We store the exact words, the time, the page address, and the IP address.
  • Phone. When a person calls +1 516-206-6682, the agent reads the script below. If the caller agrees, we record the number and send the confirmation text.

Website checkbox

I agree to receive customer care texts from Home Reno Hotspot at the number provided, including qualification questions, appointment updates, and messages from the plumber connected through Home Reno Hotspot. Phone script

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive customer care texts from Home Reno Hotspot about your water heater request, including scheduling updates and messages from the plumber connected through Home Reno Hotspot. Message frequency may vary. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. We will not share your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

The confirmation text is: You have agreed to receive SMS updates from Home Reno Hotspot. Msg freq may vary. Std msg & data rates apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Opt-Out (Unsubscribe)

Homeowners can opt out of SMS at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message from Home Reno Hotspot. We will send a final confirmation and cease SMS to that number.
  • Emailing us: help@homerenohotspot.com or privacy@homerenohotspot.com to request removal from SMS communications.

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Our full SMS and data practices are described in our Privacy Policy.

Documentation and Compliance

We retain the consent record, including the words shown or spoken and the time, for compliance. For questions, contact privacy@homerenohotspot.com or see our Contact page.
