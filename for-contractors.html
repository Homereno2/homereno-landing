For Contractors

Licensed plumbers in Columbus. One water heater job at a time.

Home Reno Hotspot introduces homeowners who need a water heater replaced to one licensed plumber. You pay a referral fee only after the homeowner confirms you. Planned jobs are $75–$100. Urgent jobs, a leak or no hot water, are $125–$150. The exact fee is in the offer.

What you receive

  • One offer at a time, for your zip codes and heater types.
  • Heater details from the label photo, the problem, and the address.
  • A conversation through a Home Reno Hotspot number after the homeowner confirms you.
  • An automatic refund if you are not able to make contact inside the response window, or if the homeowner was not the owner or was outside the zip code.

What we look for

An active Ohio plumbing license, current insurance, and a Google Business Profile that meets the standards on the partner site.
