Introduction

Home Reno Hotspot is a service of Home Reno Hotspot LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”). We introduce homeowners in Columbus, Ohio who need a water heater replaced to one licensed plumber at a time. This Privacy Policy describes what personal information we collect, how we use it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

This policy applies when you use our website, call us, or receive text messages from us. Questions may be directed to the contact information at the end of this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to qualify a water heater request, check a plumber, and make one introduction:

  • Contact information: Name, phone number, and email address when you provide them.
  • Request details: Zip code, whether you own the home, whether the heater is leaking, out of hot water, or a planned replacement, and the timing you prefer.
  • Label photo: A photo of the water heater label, when you choose to send one, and the brand, model, fuel, size, and age we read from it.
  • Communication data: The content and time of calls and text messages with us and, after you confirm a plumber, messages relayed through our number.
  • Consent records: Whether you agreed to texts, the exact words shown or read to you, when you agreed, and, on the website, the page address and IP address.

Label photos

If you send a photo of the water heater label, we use it to read the brand, model, fuel, size, and year. After you confirm a plumber, that plumber receives the photo and those details, plus your first name and a Home Reno Hotspot relay number. We do not give the plumber your real phone number unless you choose to give it.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Qualify water heater requests and decide whether a request can be offered to a plumber.
  • Read a label photo when you provide one.
  • Verify a plumber’s license and insurance.
  • Ask you to confirm one named plumber, then connect the conversation through our number.
  • Send customer-care texts about your request, scheduling, and that conversation.
  • Charge the plumber a referral fee after you confirm them, and refund that fee when our terms say so.
  • Respond to support requests, process STOP replies, and comply with the law.

Masked Relay Communication

After you confirm a plumber, calls and texts run through a Home Reno Hotspot number. The plumber sees that number, not your real number, unless you choose to give your number. Those conversations may be recorded.

Text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared with the specific plumbing partner assigned to your request solely to complete your service.

When you opt in to texts from Home Reno Hotspot:

  • Consent: You can opt in on our website with an optional checkbox, or by agreeing when you call +1 516-206-6682. Consent is not a condition of submitting a request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP to any message to opt out. We send a final confirmation and then stop texts. Opting out does not delete the rest of your request record. See “Your Rights” below.
  • Help: Reply HELP or call +1 516-206-6682.
  • What the texts are: Qualification questions, scheduling updates, a request to confirm one named plumber, and one-to-one messages from that plumber sent through Home Reno Hotspot. We do not send marketing or promotional texts on this program.

Who receives information

With the plumber you confirm

After you confirm a plumber, we give that plumber your first name, address, label photo, and the details read from it. Contact then runs through a Home Reno Hotspot number.

With service providers

These providers process information for us and only for the purposes in this policy:

  • Telnyx — text message and call delivery, including the relay between you and a plumber.
  • Airtable — the record of your request, consent, and the introduction.
  • Stripe — the plumber’s referral fee. Stripe does not receive your label photo.
  • Website hosting — this website.

These providers are contractually required to protect your information and use it only to perform services for us, except where they must retain data under their own legal or security obligations.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of our platform and users.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time (see “Your Rights”). We will process deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and operational requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements.
  • Opt out of texts by replying STOP to any message.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting prior lawful processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

Our service is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.
