Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Home Reno Hotspot LLC (“we”) operates Home Reno Hotspot, a referral and booking platform. We do not perform plumbing work. Water heater replacement is performed by independent licensed contractors.

What we collect

When you request a water heater replacement or sign up as a plumbing partner, we collect your name, phone number, address, water heater details and photos, and messages. Partner sign-ups also include a business name, email, license number, and service zip codes.

How we use it

We use this information to coordinate water heater replacement requests and partner job offers. That includes asking follow-up questions, sharing a request with the specific plumbing partner assigned to it, and sending texts you agreed to receive.

Text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared with the specific plumbing partner assigned to your request solely to complete your service.

How long we keep it

We keep request records, messages, photos, and consent records while a request or partnership is active and afterward as needed to answer questions, handle disputes, and meet legal recordkeeping duties. You can email us to ask about information we are not required to keep.
